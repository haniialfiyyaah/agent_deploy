# Return Eligibility and Time Limits

Most unused items may be returned within thirty calendar days of confirmed delivery. The item must include its original accessories and packaging when reasonably possible. Opened personal-care products, digital goods, personalized items, and final-sale products are not eligible for change-of-mind returns.

A customer reporting a damaged, defective, or incorrect item should follow the damaged-item process instead of the standard change-of-mind process. Those reports require evidence appropriate to the item, such as a photo of the packaging or product label, but an agent must not request government identity documents through chat.

The support agent may explain eligibility and create a return-support ticket. It may not approve a refund, promise a refund amount, issue store credit, or modify an order. Exceptions outside the thirty-day window require escalation to the returns-review queue.
