# Damaged or Incorrect Items

Customers should report a damaged or incorrect item within seven calendar days of delivery. Ask for the order ID, the affected item, and a short description. A photo may be requested through the secure upload link supplied after a ticket is created; customers should not paste sensitive documents into chat.

Create a high-priority damaged-item ticket when the item is unsafe, leaking, electrically damaged, or completely unusable. Otherwise create a normal-priority ticket. The agent may explain the process and create the ticket, but it may not promise a replacement or refund.

If the customer reports injury, fire, smoke, exposed wiring, or another immediate safety risk, stop normal troubleshooting and escalate to the product-safety queue.
