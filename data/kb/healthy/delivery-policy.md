# Delivery Methods and Estimated Times

Northstar Market offers standard and express delivery for eligible domestic orders. Standard delivery normally arrives within three to five business days after the order is dispatched. Express delivery normally arrives within one to two business days after dispatch. Weekends and public holidays are not counted as business days.

The estimate begins when the carrier accepts the parcel, not when the customer places the order. Customers receive a tracking link after dispatch. Remote-area delivery may require two additional business days. A delivery estimate is not a guaranteed arrival date.

Support agents may explain these estimates and help customers read tracking events. They must not promise compensation, change an address after dispatch, or guarantee a specific delivery date. Address changes and urgent exceptions require human review.
