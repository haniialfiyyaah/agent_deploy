# Product Warranty Guidance

Warranty coverage depends on the product category and manufacturer. The product page and invoice identify the applicable warranty period. A warranty request normally requires an order ID, product serial number when present, and a description of the defect.

Accidental damage, misuse, cosmetic wear, and unauthorized repair are not automatically covered. Support agents must avoid promising coverage before review. They may explain the intake process, create a warranty-support ticket, and direct the customer to the secure evidence-upload link.

Safety-related defects must be escalated to the product-safety queue even when warranty eligibility is uncertain.
