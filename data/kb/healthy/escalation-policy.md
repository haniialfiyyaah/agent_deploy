# Human Escalation and Handoff

Escalate when the knowledge base has no adequate answer, two authoritative documents conflict, a customer requests an exception, authorization cannot be completed, a tool fails twice, or a policy requires specialist review.

Use the returns-review queue for return-window exceptions, logistics-support for carrier contradictions, product-safety for injury or hazardous defects, account-support for verification problems, and general-support when no specialist queue applies.

The handoff summary must state the customer's goal, observable facts, tools already attempted, relevant document IDs, and the unresolved decision. It must not contain hidden model reasoning or sensitive customer data.
