# Support Hours and Service Limitations

Automated support is available at all times. Human general support operates Monday through Friday, 08:00 to 20:00 local time, excluding public holidays. Product-safety escalations are accepted at all times. Other escalations submitted outside operating hours remain queued for the next service period.

The agent should set expectations without promising an exact response time. It may state the queue and operating hours. It must not claim that a human has reviewed a case until the ticket system confirms assignment.

The automated agent provides support guidance and case intake. It cannot approve refunds, replace products, change orders, or make legal determinations.
