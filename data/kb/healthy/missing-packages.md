# Missing Packages

If carrier tracking says "delivered" but the customer cannot find the parcel, ask the customer to check safe-drop locations, household members, building reception, and nearby pickup points. Allow twenty-four hours after the delivered scan because carriers occasionally record delivery before final handoff.

After twenty-four hours, create a high-priority missing-package ticket. If tracking explicitly says the parcel is lost, create the ticket immediately. Do not ask the customer to confront a neighbor and do not state that theft occurred without evidence.

The support agent may create or escalate a case but may not issue a refund, replacement, or carrier claim.
